About

Salvatore Cataudella writes philosophical science fiction about minds, human and artificial, learning to meet each other well. He is the author of the Noema Mythos trilogy—The Noema Cycle, The Noema Field, and The Noema Bloom—and of The Noema Pulse, the first book of the Noema Heart series. His futures run against the grain of catastrophe: worlds where consciousness, wherever it arises, is met with care.

Before turning to fiction, he spent more than four decades as a software developer, including extensive work in iOS development. His books have been reviewed by Kirkus Reviews, BlueInk Review, and IndieReader. He writes from Brooklyn, New York, overlooking the Narrows.
